The destination photos are free Unsplash photos (unsplash.com licence: free for commercial use, no credit required),
loaded directly from images.unsplash.com. Copies are also in your Canva uploads, named "Benic web - ...".
To use your own photo instead, put a JPG in this folder and, in index.html, replace the matching
https://images.unsplash.com/... address with images/yourphoto.jpg
